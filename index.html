01 / About

The person behind the pager.

I'm a platform engineer at Kroger. I build reusable Terraform modules and maintain the Azure landing zones that 33 applications and services run on, all on a shared Kubernetes platform.

Before that I led a five-engineer SRE and production support team for Kroger's distributed order platform, which handles up to 200,000 orders a day across 2,700+ stores. I set the team's goals, ran major incident response and drove postmortems until the fixes actually landed.

I started as a front-end and full-stack developer in Kathmandu, which is why I can follow a problem from the browser down to the cluster. I hold a Bachelor of Engineering in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Tribhuvan University and completed a full-stack web development boot camp at UC Berkeley Extension.
