About me

I'm a platform engineer at Kroger, where I own the reliability and day-to-day health of production systems at national scale. My career runs from hands-on full-stack development to production support, site reliability and platform engineering, so I understand a system from the browser all the way down to the cluster it runs on.

I started as a developer, first in Nepal and then in California, and moved steadily toward the operational side. I've worked at Kroger, Deloitte (as a contractor to Kroger) and Walmart, and I'm at my best when something is large, business-critical and needs someone to take real ownership of it.

I care about the unglamorous work that makes software trustworthy: clear runbooks, good observability, safe deploys and fixing root causes instead of symptoms. I hold a bachelor's degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Nepal and I'm based in California.
